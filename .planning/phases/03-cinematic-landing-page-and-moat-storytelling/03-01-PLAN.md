# Plan 03-01: Narrative Storytelling, Problem-Solution, Nutrition Showcase & Feature Registry

## Objective
Implement the feature status registry governing rollout labels, the trust strip credibility bar, the problem-solution narrative contrasting Western apps with FitKarma, and the interactive Indian nutrition showcase.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Create Typed Feature Status Registry (`src/config/features.ts`)
- Define union type `RolloutStatus = "in_beta" | "rolling_out" | "planned"`.
- Implement `FEATURE_CATALOG` mapping all documented capabilities to their approved status:
  - `in_beta`: Indian Nutrition Database, Katori & Roti Portions, Offline Local Vault.
  - `rolling_out`: Daily Intelligence Package (DIP), WhatsApp Hinglish Logging, Tadka Oil Slider.
  - `planned`: Connected Health Rails (Health Connect/HealthKit), Family Care Dashboard, Cycle-Aware Tracking.
- Export helper `getRolloutBadge(status, locale)` returning localized labels: `"In beta"` / `"बीटा में"`, `"Rolling out during beta"` / `"बीटा के दौरान जारी"`, `"Planned within the beta programme"` / `"बीटा कार्यक्रम में नियोजित"`.

### Task 2: Build Trust Strip Component (`src/components/marketing/trust-strip.tsx`)
- Implement 4 credibility pillars across a sleek horizontal grid with glass borders:
  1. 500+ Indian Dishes (Authentic regional foods & household measures)
  2. Daily Intelligence (DIP) (Contextual advice cutting through dashboard noise)
  3. Connected Health Rails (Health Connect & Apple Health data integration)
  4. Family-Aware Care (Affirmative consent-based wellbeing sharing)
- Support localized strings from message dictionary.

### Task 3: Build Problem-Solution Narrative (`src/components/marketing/problem-solution-section.tsx`)
- Section header: *"Cultural Health Intelligence"* & *"Why Health Tracking in India Needs a Re-imagining"*.
- Side-by-side comparison layout:
  - Left Card (*Conventional Tracking Apps*): Red/muted cross icons detailing Western food bias, rigid gram measures, chart overload, and isolated tracking.
  - Right Card (*The FitKarma Ecosystem*): Emerald/gold check icons detailing 500+ regional recipes, katori/roti household units, DIP actionable briefs, and consent-first family care.
- Enhanced with subtle `JaaliPattern` background.

### Task 4: Build Indian Nutrition Showcase (`src/components/marketing/nutrition-showcase.tsx`)
- Interactive demonstration of household portion measurement:
  - Katori visualizer with volume toggles (Small 100ml, Standard 150ml, Large 250ml).
  - Roti counter with whole-wheat/millet type indicators.
  - Tadka oil estimate slider (Low 1 tsp, Medium 2 tsp, High 1 tbsp ghee/oil).
  - Family Recipe Splitter illustration (Total pot ingredients divided by household member percentage).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `features.ts` strictly enforces the 3 approved rollout statuses without inventing live capabilities.
- [ ] `TrustStrip` and `ProblemSolutionSection` render responsively on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] `NutritionShowcase` visually explains Indian culinary units (katori, roti, tadka slider) with zero layout shifts.
- [ ] Full bilingual parity between `/en` and `/hi`.
- [ ] `pnpm type-check` passes with zero errors.
