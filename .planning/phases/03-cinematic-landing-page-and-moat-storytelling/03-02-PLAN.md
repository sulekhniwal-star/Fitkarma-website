# Plan 03-02: Feature Bento Grid with 3D Depth, How It Works & FAQ Accordion

## Objective
Implement the 6-card Feature Bento Grid with interactive 3D perspective frames, the 3-step How It Works timeline, and the accessible FAQ disclosure accordion.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Build Feature Bento Grid with 3D Depth (`src/components/marketing/feature-bento-grid.tsx`)
- Implement 6 asymmetric bento cards in responsive CSS Grid:
  1. **India-First Nutrition (Span 2 cols)**: Katori/roti household portions, 500+ regional dishes, cooking yield multipliers, with rollout badge `In beta`.
  2. **Daily Intelligence Package (DIP)**: Contextual morning/evening intelligent briefs cutting through dashboard noise, with rollout badge `Rolling out during beta`.
  3. **WhatsApp Conversational Logging (Span 2 cols)**: Hinglish voice & text logging interactive UI preview (*"Bhai 2 roti aur dal tadka log kar de"*), waveform animation, with rollout badge `Rolling out during beta`.
  4. **Offline-First Resilience**: Local encrypted storage (SQLCipher direction) and automatic outbox sync, with rollout badge `In beta`.
  5. **Connected Health Rails**: Android Health Connect and Apple Health integration, with rollout badge `Planned within the beta programme`.
  6. **Family-Aware Wellness**: Consent-first family dashboard and multi-generational telemetry, with rollout badge `Planned within the beta programme`.
- Incorporate `PhoneMockup3D` perspective framing on highlighted cards with permanent *"Illustrative preview"* micro-badge.

### Task 2: Build How It Works Section (`src/components/marketing/how-it-works-section.tsx`)
- 3-step linear flow with step numbers, connecting glowing lines, and bilingual copy:
  - **Step 1: Apply in 30 Seconds**: Enter name, email, and top feature priorities. No phone numbers, no medical questions.
  - **Step 2: Instant Email Verification**: Receive a passwordless verification magic link to confirm your priority in queue.
  - **Step 3: Claim Phase 1 Founder Seat**: First 1,000 verified applicants receive VIP Phase 1 confirmation. Later applicants enter the Phase 2 waitlist.

### Task 3: Build FAQ Accordion (`src/components/marketing/faq-accordion.tsx`)
- Expandable disclosure accordion for 10 key questions from `docs/03_SITEMAP_AND_COPY.md`:
  1. What is FitKarma?
  2. Who can apply for the beta?
  3. How are the first 1,000 places allocated?
  4. What happens when Phase 1 is full?
  5. Do I need to pay to apply for the beta?
  6. Which devices will be supported?
  7. Will every feature be available on the first day?
  8. How will FitKarma contact me?
  9. What information do you collect during registration?
  10. Is FitKarma a medical service? (Explicit non-clinical disclaimer).
- Accessible keyboard interaction: `Enter` and `Space` toggle; `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` attributes; smooth height transition.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Bento grid cards display correct rollout badges matching `features.ts`.
- [ ] 3D perspective phone frames in bento cards feature visible *"Illustrative preview"* badges.
- [ ] FAQ accordion toggles open/close smoothly with accessible keyboard control.
- [ ] 100% bilingual parity in English and Hindi.
- [ ] `pnpm type-check` passes with zero errors.
