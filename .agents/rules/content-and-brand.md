# FitKarma Brand and Content Rules

- Use the exact brand spelling `FitKarma` and the approved primary positioning `India's AI-powered Fitness and Wellness Ecosystem`.
- Keep copy confident, human, modern and India-aware. Do not reduce the product to a calorie counter or generic workout tracker.
- Use the product's actual differentiators: Indian nutrition and household portions, Daily Intelligence Package, low-friction logging, offline-first direction, health-platform connectivity, privacy controls and family-aware wellness—only with accurate rollout labels.
- The full source feature inventory is intended for the beta programme, with progressive delivery. Never imply every feature is available on day one.
- No invented social proof, testimonials, customer logos, press quotes, metrics, clinical results, partnerships, medical promises or awards.
- Keep clinical/preventive data separate from traditional Ayurveda/wellness concepts. Never claim diagnosis, prevention or cure unless separately reviewed and substantiated.
- English and Hindi are required for every visible UI string, email and error state. Keep language natural rather than literal machine translation; flag copy requiring native review.
- Use illustrative product mockups until real screenshots are supplied; label mockups as illustrative where needed.
