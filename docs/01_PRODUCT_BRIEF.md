# 01 — Product Brief

## Product

**FitKarma** — India's AI-powered Fitness and Wellness Ecosystem.

## Purpose of the pre-launch website

The website is the public launch surface for explaining FitKarma, demonstrating its product direction, collecting qualified beta applications and managing phased access. It is not the Flutter app, a clinical portal or a full public social network.

## Primary objectives

1. Explain the value of FitKarma clearly to Indian users.
2. Show the product's differentiating capabilities with truthful availability labels.
3. Convert visitors into verified Phase 1 beta applicants.
4. Stop Phase 1 acceptance at exactly 1,000 places.
5. Continue collecting Phase 2 waitlist applications after Phase 1 is full.
6. Learn which features matter most through a minimal priority question.
7. Give the owner a secure way to operate phases, view applications and send approved email campaigns/transactional notices.

## Audience from the master product brief

- Tier-1 urban professionals
- Tier-2/Tier-3 and lower-bandwidth users
- College students
- Families managing household health
- Corporate wellness programmes
- Premium/clinical metabolic-health users

The public website must be understandable to a first-time visitor and must not assume medical or technical knowledge.

## Core value pillars

1. **India-first nutrition:** foods, portions, regional meals and cooking context relevant to Indian households.
2. **Personal health intelligence:** Daily Intelligence Package (DIP) that surfaces relevant context and a practical next action.
3. **Low-friction logging:** in-app, offline and conversational workflows as capabilities roll out.
4. **Connected health:** OS-level data sources such as Health Connect and Apple Health, subject to actual implementation and permissions.
5. **Privacy-focused foundations:** clear controls and transparent data handling; do not make absolute or unverified security claims.
6. **Family-aware wellness:** consent-based sharing and family-oriented tools where operational and legal safeguards are ready.

## Beta programme principle

The owner's decision is that the full feature inventory in the FitKarma Master Documentation is intended to be available to beta participants. This is a **progressive beta programme commitment**, not a statement that every feature is active on day one. The team may release capabilities in waves, test with subsets of participants, refine based on feedback and hold capabilities that require clinical, legal, supplier, platform or integration readiness.

Website copy must describe the programme as a journey: early access, iterative releases, feedback and improvements. It must not promise specific dates or outcomes that have not been approved.

## Success metrics

### Primary

- Verified Phase 1 accepted count (target ceiling: 1,000)
- Visitor-to-application conversion rate
- Application-to-email-verification rate
- Phase 2 waitlist growth after Phase 1 fills

### Secondary

- Feature-priority distribution
- Verification email delivery and failure rate
- Duplicate/spam application rate
- Registration completion by language and device class (aggregate only)
- Landing-page performance and accessibility defects

### Measurement constraints

- Do not invent baseline or target conversion percentages.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.
- Use aggregate reporting and privacy-preserving analytics after owner approval.
- Do not put email addresses or personal data in analytics events or URL query strings.

## Non-goals for this website

- Rebuilding the Flutter app in React/Next.js.
- Reimplementing app health tracking or AI inference inside the marketing website.
- Collecting medical history, weight, symptoms, medications or diagnoses during beta registration.
- Enabling paid subscriptions, Razorpay checkout or in-app purchases through the pre-launch site unless separately approved.
- Buying a domain or paid third-party service automatically.
- Sending communications through anything other than email.
