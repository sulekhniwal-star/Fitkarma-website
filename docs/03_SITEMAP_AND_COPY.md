# 03 — Sitemap and Website Copy Direction

## Site map

- `/en` — English landing page
- `/hi` — Hindi landing page
- `/en/features` and `/hi/features` — grouped feature overview (may be section anchors on the landing page for MVP)
- `/en/beta` and `/hi/beta` — beta application
- `/en/privacy` and `/hi/privacy` — privacy notice
- `/en/terms` and `/hi/terms` — beta programme terms
- `/en/faq` and `/hi/faq` — FAQs (may be landing-page section initially)
- `/auth/confirm` — email verification callback; never index
- `/beta/status` — status page for the applicant's own verified email/session only; no public email lookup that leaks registration status
- `/admin` — protected admin console; no indexing

## Recommended landing-page sequence

1. Announcement strip (only when a real beta milestone/announcement exists).
2. Navigation: FitKarma logo/wordmark, Features, How it works, Privacy, FAQ, language switch, Join beta.
3. Cinematic hero: headline, short explanation, primary and secondary CTA, illustrative product visual.
4. Trust/positioning strip: India-first, AI-powered, built for everyday health routines—avoid unsupported certification claims.
5. Problem-to-value section: fragmented health information and friction in tracking; show FitKarma's joined-up approach without making medical promises.
6. Feature bento grid: Indian nutrition, Daily Intelligence Package, low-friction logging, offline-first, connected health, family-aware wellness.
7. Product walkthrough: illustrative screens or animated workflow; explicitly label as concept/illustrative until real UI assets are supplied.
8. India-first nutrition showcase: roti, dal, rice, idli, dosa, katori portions, raw/cooked and family recipe.
9. “How FitKarma works” in three or four steps.
10. Privacy-by-design section: describe real controls only; link to privacy notice.
11. Beta programme: 1,000 Phase 1 places, first-come-first-served after email verification, then Phase 2 waitlist.
12. FAQ.
13. Final CTA and footer with legal/contact links.

## Beta programme copy

**Heading:** Be part of the first 1,000.

**Body:** Join the FitKarma beta programme and help shape an India-first health and wellness experience. Phase 1 places are allocated first-come, first-served after email verification. Once the 1,000 places are filled, new verified applicants can join the Phase 2 waitlist.

**CTA:** Apply for beta access

Do not use a fake countdown timer or simulated live counter. A count must come from the accepted registration data and display a clear “verified Phase 1 places claimed” label.

## FAQ questions

- What is FitKarma?
- Who can apply for the beta?
- How are the first 1,000 places allocated?
- What happens when Phase 1 is full?
- Do I need to pay to apply? (Answer only after owner confirms beta commercial terms.)
- Which devices will be supported? (Use only owner-confirmed app compatibility.)
- Will every feature be available on the first day? (No; the beta programme includes staged rollouts and improvements.)
- How will FitKarma contact me? (Email only.)
- What information do you collect during registration?
- How can I withdraw my application or request deletion?
- Is FitKarma a medical service? (No unsupported diagnostic or treatment claims; explain approved boundary.)
